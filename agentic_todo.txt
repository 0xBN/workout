## Guardrails (the actual skill being learned)
- The building is NOT the goal. Applying the pattern to real work is.
- If I'm creating structure with no real task behind it → that's grooming, stop.
- Add a rule/command/step when something real fails, not when I imagine it might.
- "Good enough and used" beats "perfect and unused."
